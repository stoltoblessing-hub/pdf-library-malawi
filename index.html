Our Policies

1. Privacy Policy

PDF Library Malawi respects visitors' privacy. This basic website does not intentionally collect personal information through forms. If you email us, we will use your message to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information by email.

If hosting services or future website features collect information, this policy should be updated to explain what is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

2. Terms and Conditions

By using this website, you agree to use its resources lawfully and responsibly. Do not use the website to distribute harmful or unlawful material.

Download only materials you are permitted to access. You must not copy, redistribute or sell copyrighted materials without permission from the copyright owner.

3. Copyright Policy

We aim to share original materials, public-domain works, openly licensed resources and files we have permission to distribute. If you believe a resource infringes your copyright, contact us with its details so we can review it.

4. Free Download Policy

Resources marked free are offered without a download fee. Internet or mobile data charges may still apply. We cannot guarantee that every resource will always be available or free from errors.

5. Educational Disclaimer

Resources are provided for general educational purposes. Please check the suitability and accuracy of each resource before relying on it for examinations or other important work.
